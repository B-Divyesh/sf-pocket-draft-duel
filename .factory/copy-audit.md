# Copy audit

The landing page was read aloud on a 390px viewport. Before scrolling it says
what the game does, who it is for, and that **Try it with sample data** starts a
practice draft. The first-screen sentences are under 22 words.

| Landing sentence | Words | Flag |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Draft cards and duel with friends | 6 | — |
| For two to four friends who want a complete tactical game with three quick battles. | 15 | — |
| Starts a full practice draft now. | 6 | — |
| 2–4 players by room code | 5 | — |
| Fixed 18-card set | 4 | — |
| Practice starts with no account | 6 | — |
| Draft 3 cards, play 3 battles, see one result. | 10 | — |
| Friends see one shared row. | 6 | — |
| Picks stay hidden until every player locks one. | 8 | — |
| Then choose one card and one tactic for each battle. | 11 | — |
| Make a room, share its six-letter code, then all players choose in the same room. | 15 | — |
| There are no accounts, ranked ladder, chat, custom cards, random packs, or tracking. | 13 | — |

No sentence uses: leverage, seamless, effortless, robust, powerful, intuitive,
reimagine, supercharge, delightful, journey, ecosystem, or AI-powered.
